# Month 6 — Interview Mode (Weeks 21–24)

By now interviews drive the schedule, not the plan. **The rule: every real interview generates the next day's study list.** Nothing else.

**Weekday split:**

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 1.5 hr | DSA — keep warm, mixed topics, timed |
| 1.0 hr | Whatever your last interview exposed |
| 1.0 hr | Interview-specific prep for what's actually scheduled |
| 0.5 hr | Revision |

**Two mocks a week, minimum.** Speaking while coding is a separate skill from coding, and it's the one that fails people at this stage.

---

| Day | Plan |
