## If it hasn't converted by Week 28

That's a normal outcome, not a failure — hiring cycles are lumpy and 4 targets is a small sample. Diagnose honestly from your post-mortems:

| Where you're failing | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Resume screens | Positioning problem | Rewrite, get referrals, widen the target list |
| DSA rounds | Pattern recall under pressure | More timed sets, not more new problems |
| Machine coding | Speed, not knowledge | More 90-min builds, no lookups |
| System design | Structure, not depth | A repeatable framework, out loud, recorded |
| HM rounds | Narrative | Rewrite the story of why you're leaving and what you own |

Extend by 6–8 weeks against the specific failure. Do not restart the plan, and do not add a second project.
